# Brand Strategy

Source: `brands/toan/strategy/brand-strategy.md`, `brands/toan/strategy/content-authority-map.md`, `brands/toan/.agents/product-marketing.md`

## Brand Objective

Build durable authority for Toan as a senior product engineer and technical lead who can turn ambiguous requirements into production systems, while keeping Aitbase Camera awareness separate from the rest of the personal brand.

## Audience Priority

1. SaaS founders
2. Engineering leaders
3. Product-focused technology companies
4. Recruiters hiring senior full-stack or product engineers
5. International remote engineering teams

## Differentiation

- One coherent engineering story across software, cloud, AI, search, and hardware-adjacent work.
- Evidence of production ownership, not just prototypes.
- Strong fit for workflow-heavy products where architecture, data, and operations matter.
- Leadership evidence plus solo product ownership.

## Conversion Intent

- Primary conversion action: read a case study.
- Secondary paths: view selected projects, start a conversation, or inspect the proof behind a claim.

## Channel Roles

- LinkedIn: credibility and decision-maker trust.
- X: build logs, architecture trade-offs, and short technical insights.
- GitHub: technical proof and documentation.
- Personal website: canonical profile, durable case studies, portfolio, and contact.

## Content Guardrails

- Use the proof register before publishing strong claims or metrics.
- Keep Omnichannel proof attached to Omnichannel.
- Keep Camera discovery-stage until Camera-specific evidence exists.
- Do not blur private research into public copy.
