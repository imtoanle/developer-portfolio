# Website CRO Review

Source: `brands/toan/strategy/website-cro-review.md`

## Executive Assessment

The website draft set is structurally sound for the intended audiences. The homepage communicates who Toan is, what he is strong at, and why the experience is credible early in the visit.

There are no major conversion blockers in the current draft set.

## Primary Conversion Path

1. Homepage
2. Projects index
3. Relevant project summary
4. Contact

For cold visitors, the projects page is the right proof-first step. For visitors who already have a fit in mind, the contact page is the right low-friction action.

## Audience-Specific Paths

- SaaS founders: homepage -> FinderLab or AI OCR.
- Engineering leaders: homepage -> AI OCR, Octan, or JobReady proof.
- Product-focused companies: homepage -> projects index -> relevant project.
- Recruiters: homepage -> FinderLab and the proof bullets.

## Gaps

- No dedicated CV route yet.
- No full case study pages yet.
- Trust is good, but deeper proof would help.

## Recommended Next Steps

- Add a visible CV route for recruiters.
- Create at least one FinderLab case study and one AI OCR case study.
- Keep the current project navigation cues on the projects page.
- Keep the secondary contact CTA in the homepage hero.
