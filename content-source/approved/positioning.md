## One-line Positioning

Senior Product Engineer building production software, AI workflow systems, and software-enabled hardware products.

## Public-Safe Narrative

Toan Le is a senior full-stack product engineer and technical lead with 10+ years of software engineering experience and approximately seven years of professional Ruby on Rails experience.

He builds systems that need strong architecture, clean data models, search, automation, and reliable operations. The common thread is turning ambiguous requirements into production systems that can be shipped, operated, and evolved.

Aitbase Omnichannel is CV-supported product work. Aitbase Camera is a separate discovery-stage product and must remain separate from Omnichannel proof.

## Positioning Guardrails

- Keep the brand anchored in production systems, not tool inventory.
- Keep FinderLab scale tied to FinderLab only.
- Keep Aitbase Camera separate from Aitbase Omnichannel.
- Do not promote unsupported metrics, customer names, or revenue claims.
