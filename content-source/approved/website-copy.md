## About

### What I Do

I build production systems that have to work in the real world. That usually means product engineering, architecture, database design, workflow logic, and the operational details that decide whether software stays useful after launch.

### Background

I'm a senior full-stack product engineer and technical lead with 10+ years of software engineering experience and around seven years of professional Ruby on Rails experience.

The strongest pattern across my work is ownership. At FinderLab, I built and operated a solo product. At JobReady, I led a team of 5-7 engineers and owned architecture and database decisions. At FPT Blockchain Lab and Octan Network, I worked on blockchain infrastructure and technical delivery. At Aitbase, I built Aitbase Omnichannel as a support automation product with shared inbox, messaging integrations, AI-assisted replies, and human handoff.

### How I Think About Engineering

I care about the parts that make a system durable:

- clear boundaries
- sane data models
- search and analytics that stay usable
- automation that does not break human oversight
- trade-offs that are explicit enough for other engineers to maintain

### What I'm Looking For

I'm at my best in senior product engineering and technical lead roles where the work needs judgment, not just output. Consulting conversations are also a fit when the problem is architecture, workflow design, or production AI.

### FinderLab

Solo-built B2B contact discovery and lead generation SaaS.

FinderLab is the clearest proof of end-to-end product ownership. I built and operated it as a solo product, and the searchable dataset exceeded 600 million profile records.

### AI OCR & Document Processing Platform

AI-assisted document intake and review workflow.

This platform handled vehicle dossier workflows with OCR extraction, duplicate detection, validation rules, audit logging, review and override flows, and submission package generation. It used Cloudflare Workers, D1, R2, queues, and React-based admin tools.

### Aitbase Camera

Discovery-stage desktop AI camera assistant under the Aitbase umbrella.

Aitbase Camera is a separate product from Aitbase Omnichannel. It is still in discovery, so the right way to read it is as a founder experiment and a software-enabled hardware product exploration.

### Aitbase Omnichannel

CV-supported support automation product.

Aitbase Omnichannel included a shared inbox, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp integrations, AI-assisted replies, automation, and human-agent handoff.

### Octan Network Reputation Scoring

Blockchain reputation scoring and analytics.

This project covered backend services, multi-chain data processing, scoring logic, APIs, and platform features for reputation analytics.
